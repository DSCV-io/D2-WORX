Candidate predicate: "an `AddD2X` composition test resolves every public seam it registers."
- **Multi-replica startup ensure-db must treat BOTH `23505` (unique_violation) AND `42P04` (duplicate_database) as the benign concurrent-create outcome** — a single-SqlState catch crash-loops on the more-likely race timing (WP-2 NF-1). The §9.9 startup-migration pattern.
